# How to Access Logs

Use this when you need to review the logs to determine an issue or provide them to a member of the Orchid team. For a crash, *Help > Report a Crash on GitHub...* opens a bug report with the log excerpt already filled in.

---

## Pre-Requisites

- Orchid installed and run at least once

---

## Steps

###  Windows

*By default the logs are stored in your AppData `C:\Users\<USER>\AppData\Roaming\orchid\log` (`...\Roaming\eden\log` if you used the emulator before it had its own folder), or the local **user** folder if you have a portable installation.*

<aside>

1. Open Orchid.
2. Navigate to *File > Open Orchid Folders > Log Folder*, a Windows explorer window will appear.
3. Your log files are now here for your review or upload.
</aside>

### Steam Deck

*By default the logs are stored in `/home/deck/.local/share/orchid/log` (`/home/deck/.local/share/eden/log` if you used the emulator before it had its own folder), or the local **user** folder if you have a portable installation.*

<aside>

1. Open Orchid.
2. Navigate to *File > Open Orchid Folders > Log Folder*, a Dolphin file explorer window will appear.
3. Your log files are now here for your review or upload.
</aside>

### Android

*Logs are stored in the application data, so you wouldn't be able to access the files directly without a rooted device.*

<aside>

1. Open the Orchid application on your device.
2. Navigate to *Settings > Share Debug Logs.* You can either save it to your device or send it through some other manner supported by your device.
</aside>
